The token count (his "a percent and a token count"; the raw count of all tokens, cache re-reads included): a worker thread (`src/main/token-scan-worker.ts`, built by Forge as `.vite/build/token-scan-worker.cjs` and run by `token-scan-host.ts` with `eval`) keeps a `TokenScanner` (`src/main/token-scan.ts`) that reads `~/.claude/projects/**/*.jsonl` (`PATTON_CLAUDE_PROJECTS_DIR` override) incrementally by byte offset, one request per (`message.id`, `requestId`), files and requests since the scan floor only; `src/shared/token-credit.ts` credits each request to the login live at its time by accounts.json's `switches` (the last switch at or before it), counts it in that login's current week, marks the count complete only when the first switch is at or before the week's start (else partial, from the first switch), and estimates the week as used and allowance (`weekEstimate`; 26 Nov 2026, his "it should just be 1.2 out of 5 ... really simple"): a complete count is allowance = tokens ÷ percent × 100 and used = tokens, from 5% shown; a partial one is allowance = tokens ÷ (percent at the scan − p0) × 100 and used = percent at the scan × allowance, where p0 is the week's percent at the first switch, the highest reading at or before it in the status line's `seven-day.log` (written by `statusline.sh`, beside accounts.json; `src/shared/seven-day-log.ts`, keyed by resets_at only because its id column can name the wrong login; 2 MB cap, re-read when its mtime or size changes and at each scan, where p0 is captured with the count and the percent so the whole estimate is fixed per scan; a read problem keeps the last good readings, and a log never readable gives the reason `log-unusable`), and only from a 10-point rise (whole-percent readings make 5 points up to 20% wrong). The estimate is drawn when a scan ends, from the percent then, and held until the next good scan (a percent that moves between scans must not move the allowance); with none, `weekTokens.noEstimate` says why (nothing counted, under 5%, no reading of the week at the count's start, too little rise) and the hover words it. No estimate, no token figure on the line. `plan-usage-ipc.ts` scans every 60 s (on the 10 s poll) and at once when the file's weeks or switches change, and joins each count into the push as `weekTokens` (shown only against the week it was counted for); `usage-format.ts` words it ("1.9 of 5.7 bn · resets Wed 01:00", both figures in one unit to 2 significant figures, never "≥", "~" or "since"), and the tooltip, accessible name and rail tooltip say it is an estimate and from what ("about 5.6 of 5.7 bn tokens this week, estimated from 2.1 bn counted since Tue 20:22 while the week rose from 61% to 97%"). The count is approximate by construction, and the words say so ("the count is what Claude Code recorded while this login was live ... approximate around switches"): the producer records a switch at the first status-line render after the login changes, so requests of a session still on the old login, and of `claude -p` runs (which render no status line), are credited to the new one, and a switch that never rendered is missing from `switches` while the count still reads "complete" (its first switch at or before the week's start). Files are re-read from the start when their bytes before the saved offset no longer hash the same (an in-place rewrite); a request seen in two files counts once, at its earliest time; no transcripts folder (or none in it) gives no count, not a 0; a count older than 5 minutes (scans failing) says "tokens counted at HH:MM" in its tooltip. A first scan of 5.1 GB / 1,913 files took 3.2 s (warm cache), a later one 0.14 s. Logs: `[USAGE] read {accounts, live}`, `[USAGE] no data {reason}`, `[USAGE] tokens {accounts, totalsM, files, read, skipped, bytes, requests, ms}` (only when the accounts or a total in rounded millions change), `[USAGE] tokens scan failed {error}` (once per distinct error), `[USAGE] tokens read failed {file, code}` (once per distinct file and code), `[USAGE] tokens worker restarted {starts}`, `[USAGE] estimate {id, method, usedB, allowanceB, dPct, reason}` (when a login's estimate to 2 figures changes), `[USAGE] seven-day log unusable {reason, keptReadings}` and `[USAGE] seven-day log lines skipped {skipped, readings}` (once per change) (main), `[USAGE] panel {accounts, live}` (renderer; token counts are left out of `usageIdentity`, so they log only in main).
