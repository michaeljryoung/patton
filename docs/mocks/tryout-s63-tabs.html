How selected tabs look

Selected tabs are the ones a ⌘-click or ⇧-click picks out so you can move, group or flag them together. The tab you're on keeps its look and counts as one of them. In each strip betterterm is the tab you're on and three more are selected: ai-brain, outreach (in the Research group) and voice.

Try it

Click a tab to go to it (that clears any selection). ⌘-click adds a tab to the selection or takes it out. ⇧-click selects every tab from the last one you clicked to this one. Esc, or a click on the empty part of the strip, clears. Right-click a selected tab for what you can do to all of them. Drag a selected tab and the whole selection moves with it, in its order. Here you can drag from anywhere on a tab; in Patton it would be the small handle that shows when you point at a tab. Closing and folding groups are off.
