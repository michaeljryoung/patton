Ten icons for Patton

Ten directions for a sleeker icon that keeps the reference to the general: his helmet, his three stars, his name, and the tank named after him. Each sits on the rounded tile that current Mac apps use, shown full size and at 64, 32 and 16 pixels, three of the sizes Patton's icon file carries.

Round 2 · A, less curved

You picked A and said the helmet shouldn't be too curved. Here are four flatter versions beside the first: a flatter brim, a flatter crown, both, and a straight brim.

A1As first drawn

The rim sweeps well down at the sides, under a round crown.

A2Flatter brim

The same crown; the rim drops half as far at the sides.

A3Flatter crown

A lower, broader top with steeper sides; the first rim.

A4Both flatter

The lower crown on the flatter brim.

A5Straight brim

A flat-topped crown on a straight brim, the most geometric.

Today

A fine-line pen portrait on a plain white square, which macOS 26 clips into its rounded tile. Its thin lines fade at small sizes.

AHelmet

Today's helmet and three stars, without the face. The nearest to what you have now.

Keeps the helmet, the three stars

BSilhouette

The general in profile as one solid shape, like the head on a medal.

Keeps the portrait, the helmet, the stars

CUnder the brim

The helmet drawn as a single line, with a terminal prompt where the face was.

Keeps the helmet, the stars, the terminal

DStar cursor

A terminal prompt whose cursor is a general's star.

Keeps a star, the terminal

EWindow stars

A terminal window whose three window buttons are the three stars.

Keeps the three stars, the terminal

FThree stars

Only the rank: three stars in brass on olive drab. The clearest at small sizes.

Keeps the three stars

GStencil star

The white star in a ring painted on American tanks in the Second World War, with stencil breaks in the ring.

Keeps his army's marking

HStencil P

A stencilled P, like the lettering on army crates, with the three stars beneath.

Keeps the name, the three stars

IDog tag

A steel identity tag stamped with the name, the stars and a prompt.

Keeps the name, the stars, the terminal

JPatton tank

The M48 Patton tank, named after him, side on with its white star.

Keeps the name, his army's marking
