Patton Look Directions

Five ways Patton could look more modern and high tech. Every mock shows the same window and the same Claude Code session; only the design changes. Pick one, or name the parts of two.

Dark mode is the primary view in each. The strip under every window shows that direction's other theme. Window chrome, the Bookmarks sidebar and the compose panel are Patton's to restyle; the terminal shows Claude's output under that direction's palette.

What Patton controls

  • Window chrome, tab bar, the Bookmarks and notes sidebars, compose panel, dialogs, settings.
  • In the terminal: background, foreground, the 16 ANSI colours, font, size, line height, padding, cursor.

What Claude Code controls

  • Its own layout and glyphs (⏺ ⎿ ◐◑ ✳ ⏵⏵), and its /theme setting (dark, light, daltonized variants).
  • Some fixed RGB tints that ignore the terminal palette. A palette changes how Claude's text reads, never its layout.

Vitrine

Translucent dark chrome with blur and vibrancy, soft depth, the window reads as part of macOS.

Chrome
Tab bar, sidebars and compose panel become frosted panes over the desktop (Electron vibrancy: 'under-window'). The terminal pane stays the most opaque part, 80%, so text keeps its contrast. A 1 px inner highlight on the window edge.
Radii
Window 12 px, tabs 7 px, sidebar rows 6 px. Softer than today's 8 px tab corners, no sharp edges anywhere.
Spacing
Unchanged from today: 38 px bar, 14 px tab padding, 5 × 8 px sidebar rows. The depth does the work, not the gaps.
Type
SF Pro 12 for chrome, SF Mono 12.5 in the terminal. Nothing new to bundle.
Motion
Tab and row selection cross-fade in 150 ms; the blur follows whatever is behind the window as you drag it. Dot pulse as now.

16 ANSI

Claude under it: the orange and permission blue sit comfortably on blue-black glass. Its inactive grey (153,153,153) only keeps 4.5:1 while the terminal pane stays near-opaque, which is why the translucency stops at the terminal edge. Dark /theme, as now.

2

Scope

Near-black, one neon accent, hairline borders and monospace UI type: an instrument panel.

Chrome
Near-black on every surface, every edge a 1 px hairline. No fills for state: the active tab carries a 2 px cyan rule along its top, the active row along its left, each with a faint glow. Nothing else lights up.
Radii
Window 4 px. Tabs, rows and the compose bar 0 to 2 px. Dots stay round, so they read as lamps.
Spacing
Tighter: 34 px tab bar, 12 px tab padding, 4 × 8 px rows. The denser grid is what makes it feel like a panel rather than a document.
Type
JetBrains Mono for the chrome as well as the terminal, 11.5 px. Group names and the compose hint set in 10 px uppercase with 0.1 em tracking.
Motion
None except the needs-you pulse and a 120 ms glow on focus. No easing anywhere; instruments switch, they don't slide.
chrome surface accent term bg term fg
16 ANSI

Claude under it: the chrome has one colour, so Claude's orange becomes the only warm thing on screen, which is the point. Its permission blue (177,185,249) sits near the cyan accent and reads as the same family. The inactive grey holds 7:1 on this black. Dark /theme.

3

Graphite

Flat charcoal, 5 px radii, muted secondary text and crisp hierarchy, the Linear and Raycast school.

Chrome
Flat charcoal, no gradients, no glow. Tabs become 26 px pills inside the bar instead of document tabs; the active one is a shade lighter with a 1 px inset edge. The indigo accent appears only on the active project's mark, the compose bar and the cursor.
Radii
Window 10 px; tabs, rows, dialogs and inputs all 5 px. One radius, everywhere.
Spacing
38 px bar kept, 10 px tab padding, 12 px sidebar inset, 5 × 8 px rows. A 4 px unit throughout, so edges line up across panels.
Type
Geist 12 for chrome (500 weight marks the active item, no colour needed), Geist Mono 12.5 in the terminal. Secondary text drops to a muted grey so the hierarchy comes from weight and tone.
Motion
150 ms ease-out on anything that changes state. Nothing pulses except the needs-you dot, which trades its glow for a flat 3 px halo.

16 ANSI

Claude under it: the orange and the permission tint both sit on the indigo-grey terminal without clashing, and the inactive grey holds 6.6:1 on #141417. This is the smallest step from today's Patton: same layout, same tab bar height, a calmer palette and one radius. Dark /theme.

4

Ledger

Light first: warm off-white, ink text, one cobalt accent. Light can be high tech too.

Chrome
Warm off-white chrome, like ledger paper; the active tab is the same paper as the terminal, so page and tab join without a seam. One cobalt accent: the active project's rule, the compose bar, the cursor. Dots lose their glow for a thin tinted ring, because halos go muddy on light.
Radii
Window 10 px, tabs 6 px, rows 6 px.
Spacing
As today: 38 px bar, 14 px tab padding, 5 × 8 px rows. Light surfaces need no extra air; the contrast carries the hierarchy.
Type
Instrument Sans 12 for chrome, IBM Plex Mono 12.5 in the terminal. Ink text #1d1c19, warm secondary greys, never a cold grey on cream.
Motion
A 200 ms underline slide between tabs; the dot pulse stays. Everything else is still.
chrome surface accent term bg term fg
16 ANSI

Claude under it: this one needs /theme light (or light-daltonized) in Claude Code. Its dark theme's inactive grey (153,153,153) is 2.8:1 on cream and the prompt box all but vanishes; the light theme's values (inactive 102, success 44/122/57, error 171/43/63) are what is shown here. Claude's orange is the same in both themes and reads as a warm accent on paper.

5

Phosphor

Dense, zero radius, amber and green phosphor accents: a retro-future instrument.

Chrome
A dense black panel. Tabs are 30 px cells divided by hairlines, the active one amber with a 2 px rule along its bottom, like a tuner. Sidebar labels uppercase and tracked. The terminal carries faint 3 px scanlines at 2.5%, enough to feel like glass, not enough to touch the text.
Radii
0 everywhere. Dots and the parked ring stay round, so they read as lamps against the straight edges.
Spacing
The densest of the five: 30 px bar, 3 × 10 px rows, 12 px terminal type at 1.4 line height. About a fifth more lines on screen than today.
Type
Share Tech Mono 11 for chrome, uppercase. IBM Plex Mono 12 in the terminal, because Share Tech has one weight and few glyphs, and Claude's ⏺ ⎿ ◐ need a full mono.
Motion
The cursor blinks in steps. Nothing eases; the needs-you dot pulses in phosphor green. That is all.
chrome surface accent needs you term bg term fg
16 ANSI

Claude under it: Claude's orange and the amber accent are near neighbours, so the ◐ spinner reads as part of the panel. The flag yellow is a neighbour too, and only its dot shape separates it from the accent; that is the one cost of this direction. Permission blue stands out hard against the warm panel, which for a permission prompt is useful. Dark /theme.

Reading the mocks

Tab states are the real ones: a pulsing green dot means Claude stopped and needs you, a static yellow dot is a flag you set, a hollow ring is new and means the tab is parked, waiting on its own background agent. The ◐◑ spinner alternates about every second, as Claude Code's does. Fonts are from Google Fonts here; the shipped app would bundle them.
